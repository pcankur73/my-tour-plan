⛰️ Ultimate North Sikkim Expedition

8 Days Offbeat & Iconic Adventure Tour Plan

📍 From: Garia, Kolkata 👥 Group Size: 6 Persons 💰 Budget: ₹15,000 / Head

Day 1: Departure from Kolkata

Garia ➔ Sealdah ➔ Overnight Train

Board an auto/metro from Garia to Sealdah Railway Station. Catch the 12377 Padatik Express (11:20 PM) or 12343 Darjeeling Mail (10:05 PM) heading towards New Jalpaiguri (NJP).

🚂 Transport Sleeper / 3AC Class Train (Reserve tickets 120 days prior).
🍿 Food & Snacks Dinner packed from home or Sealdah station food court (Hot Kati Rolls / Thali).
🏡 Lodging Overnight Train Journey.

Day 2: NJP to Gangtok & Local Exploration

NJP ➔ Gangtok (120 km / 4.5 hrs)

Reach NJP around 8:00 AM. Board a pre-booked reserved Bolero/Sumo to Gangtok. Check into your hotel, refresh, and visit Gangtok MG Marg in the evening for food and permit submissions.

📍 Major Attractions
  • Teesta River View Point (En route)
  • MG Marg Evening Walk
  • Ganesh Tok (Sunset view)
🍴 Fooding & Snacks Breakfast at Siliguri Junction; Dinner at Roll House / Nimtho (MG Marg) for authentic Sikkimese Thukpa & Momos.
🏨 Hotel Accommodation Budget Stay near MG Marg / Development Area (e.g., Hotel Hill Point or similar).

Day 3: Gangtok to Lachen (The Gateway to North)

Gangtok ➔ Lachen (120 km / 6 hrs)

Start early around 9:00 AM in a dedicated Mahindra Bolero/Sumo (Exclusive North Sikkim Permit vehicle). Drive through dramatic waterfalls and high mountain rivers.

📍 Attractions & Offbeat Spots
  • Seven Sister Waterfalls
  • Naga Waterfalls
  • Chungthang Confluence (Teesta & Lachen River)
  • Singhik Viewpoint (Kanchenjunga View)
🍴 Fooding Lunch at Mangan local eatery (Simple Rice-Dal-Sabzi included in North Sikkim Package); Tea & Snacks at Chungthang.
🏡 Homestay / Lodging Lachen Local Wooden Homestay (Includes Dinner: Veg/Non-Veg Thali).

Day 4: Gurudongmar Lake, Kala Patthar & Reach Lachung

Lachen ➔ Gurudongmar ➔ Kala Patthar ➔ Lachung

Early morning departure at 4:00 AM. Ascend to one of the highest altitude lakes in the world through cold desert terrain of Thangu.

📍 Highlights & Hidden Places
  • Gurudongmar Lake (17,800 ft): Holy, crystal-blue lake.
  • Thangu Valley: Cold high-altitude village.
  • Kala Patthar (Hidden Gem): Snow-covered black mountains.
  • Chopta Valley view.
🍴 Fooding & Snacks Breakfast at Thangu (Hot Maggi, Butter Tea, Bread-Omelette); Lunch at Lachen homestay; Dinner at Lachung homestay.
🏡 Lodging Traditional Wooden Hotel / Homestay at Lachung.

Day 5: Yumthang Valley, Zero Point / Katao to Gangtok

Lachung ➔ Yumthang ➔ Zero Point ➔ Gangtok

Drive to the famous Valley of Flowers, explore high snow fields near the Indo-China border, and return back to Gangtok by evening.

📍 Highlights & Hidden Places
  • Yumthang Valley: Rhododendron sanctuary.
  • Zero Point (Yumesamdong - 15,300 ft): Permanent snow zone.
  • Mt. Katao (Offbeat Alternative): Scenic snow adventure point.
  • Sikkim Hot Springs.
🍴 Fooding Snacks at Yumthang market (Hot Momos & Tea); Late lunch at Phodong/Chungthang; Dinner at MG Marg, Gangtok.
🏨 Hotel Accommodation Budget Stay in Gangtok.

Day 6: Silk Route Extension / Offbeat Reshi & Aritar

Gangtok ➔ Tsomgo Lake ➔ Baba Mandir ➔ Gangtok

Day excursion to the high altitude glacial lake near the Indo-China border, followed by a visit to the historic shrine.

📍 Major Attractions
  • Tsomgo (Changu) Lake: Glacial alpine lake.
  • New Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir
  • Nathula Pass (Optional / Subject to permit cost)
🍴 Fooding Breakfast at Gangtok; Hot snacks (Soup, Maggi, Tibetan Bread) at Changu Market; Dinner at Gangtok local market.
🏨 Hotel Accommodation Night stay at Gangtok.

Day 7: Gangtok to NJP Station

Gangtok ➔ Namchi (Optional stop) ➔ NJP

Checkout from Gangtok by 10:00 AM. Drive back toward NJP via scenic Teesta valley. Catch the evening return train back to Kolkata (e.g., Padatik Express at 9:00 PM or Uttarbanga Express).

📍 Sights En Route Teesta Bazar, Coronation Bridge, and Souvenir shopping at Siliguri.
🍴 Fooding & Snacks Lunch at Teesta Bazar (River-side dhaba); Dinner on train/packed from NJP station.
🚂 Transport Reserved Bolero/Sumo from Gangtok to NJP + Overnight Train to Sealdah.

Day 8: Arrival in Kolkata (Garia)

Sealdah Station ➔ Garia

Reach Sealdah station early morning (~6:30 AM). Take a direct Metro or Auto back home to Garia with unforgettable memories of Sikkim!

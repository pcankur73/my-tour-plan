⛰️ Silk Route Expedition (Sikkim & West Bengal)

6 Days Offbeat Scenic Circuit Tour Plan (Optimized for Women Safety & Comfort)

📅 Dates: 19/10/2026 – 24/10/2026 👥 Group Size: 6 Persons (All Girls Group) 💰 Budget: ₹7,000 / Head

Day 1: Arrival at NJP to Icche Gaon

NJP / Siliguri ➔ Kalimpong ➔ Icche Gaon (88 km / 4 hrs)

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 08:00 AM - Pickup from NJP Railway Station by reserved vehicle. 10:30 AM - Short breakfast/tea break near Kalimpong bypass. 12:30 PM - Arrival at Icche Gaon, check-in, and document verification. 01:30 PM - Traditional lunch. 04:00 PM - Silent Valley sunset and pine forest village walk. 07:30 PM - Dinner and overnight stay.

📍 Highlights & Accommodation
  • Hotel/Homestay: Pine View Eco Homestay, Icche Gaon (Verified safe for women, proper hot water geysers, purified drinking water, 24/7 room service).
  • Check-in / Check-out: Check-in: 12:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM next day.
  • Silent Valley sunset views & Kanchenjunga panorama.
🍴 Meals & Lodging Lunch, Evening Tea & Snacks, Dinner included. Lodging at Pine View Eco Homestay.

Day 2: Icche Gaon to Sillery Gaon & Reshikhola

Icche Gaon ➔ Sillery Gaon ➔ Pedong ➔ Reshikhola (45 km / 3 hrs)

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 05:30 AM - Sunrise viewing. 08:00 AM - Breakfast and check-out. 09:00 AM - Drive to Sillery Gaon, trek to Ramitey Dara viewpoint. 11:30 AM - Explore Damsang Fort ruins. 01:00 PM - Lunch at Pedong. 03:00 PM - Descend to Reshikhola river valley, check-in, and relax by the riverside. 08:00 PM - Dinner.

📍 Highlights & Accommodation
  • Hotel/Homestay: Reshi River Eco Resort / Homestay, Reshikhola (Gated secure riverside property, reliable hot water arrangement, safe environment for girls).
  • Check-in / Check-out: Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 10:00 AM next day.
  • Ramitey Dara Viewpoint & Reshi River banks.
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast at Icche Gaon; Lunch, Snacks, & Dinner at Reshikhola.

Day 3: Reshikhola to Padamchen via Rongli

Reshikhola ➔ Rongli ➔ Kuekhola Waterfalls ➔ Padamchen (40 km / 2.5 hrs)

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 08:00 AM - Breakfast and check-out. 09:30 AM - Reach Rongli Market for inner-line permit processing (Driver assists with documentation). 11:30 AM - Depart from Rongli through Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary. 12:30 PM - Stop at Kuekhola Waterfalls. 02:00 PM - Arrival at Padamchen, check-in, and rest. 07:30 PM - Dinner.

📍 Highlights & Accommodation
  • Hotel/Homestay: Silk Route Valley Resort / Padamchen Retreat (Secure homestay managed by local families, secure compound, continuous hot water supply, bottled drinking water provided).
  • Check-in / Check-out: Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 09:00 AM next day.
  • Rongli Permit Office & Kuekhola Waterfalls.
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Padamchen Homestay.

Day 4: Padamchen to Zuluk & High Passes

Padamchen ➔ Zuluk ➔ Thambi ➔ Lungthung ➔ Nathang ➔ Kupup (60 km excursion)

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 06:00 AM - Early morning departure to beat high-altitude cloud cover. 07:30 AM - Thambi View Point (32-curve Zig-Zag Road). 09:00 AM - Lungthung & Tukla Valley. 10:30 AM - Old Baba Mandir visit. 12:00 PM - Kupup Lake (Elephant Lake) & highest golf course. 02:00 PM - Return drive to Zuluk homestay for late lunch. 07:00 PM - Warm dinner and acclimatization rest.

📍 Highlights & Accommodation
  • Hotel/Homestay: Zuluk Himalayan Crest Homestay (Trusted safety record for all-women travelers, hot water bags/geysers available, proper insulated rooms).
  • Check-in / Check-out: Check-in: 02:30 PM | Check-out: 09:00 AM next day.
  • Thambi View Point, Zig-Zag Road, & Old Baba Mandir.
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Zuluk Homestay.

Day 5: Zuluk to Nathang Valley & Snow Point Excursion

Zuluk ➔ Nathang Valley ➔ Eagle's Nest / Snow Point ➔ Padamchen

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 07:00 AM - Breakfast and checkout from Zuluk. 08:30 AM - Drive up to Nathang Valley high-altitude plains. 10:30 AM - Visit Eagle's Nest Bunker viewpoint for expansive mountain vistas. 01:00 PM - Lunch at Nathang/Zuluk. 03:00 PM - Return drive descending safely back to Padamchen for a comfortable lower-altitude night stay. 07:30 PM - Dinner.

📍 Highlights & Accommodation
  • Hotel/Homestay: Silk Route Valley Resort, Padamchen (Repeat stay for safer, lower altitude oxygen levels during sleep).
  • Check-in / Check-out: Check-in: 04:00 PM | Check-out: 09:00 AM next day.
  • Nathang Valley & Eagle's Nest Bunker viewpoint.
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Padamchen Homestay.

Day 6: Padamchen to NJP Station

Padamchen ➔ Rongli ➔ Rangpo ➔ NJP (100 km / 4.5 hrs)

Detailed Timing Adjusted Roadmap: 08:00 AM - Breakfast and final checkout. 09:00 AM - Begin return journey via Rongli and Rangpo checkpost. 01:00 PM - Lunch break near Sevoke / Teesta River. 03:30 PM - Drop off at NJP Railway Station for evening train connections home.

📍 Sights En Route Teesta River Valley, Rangpo Checkpost, Coronation Bridge.
🍴 Meals Breakfast at Homestay; Lunch on the way near Rangpo / Teesta Bazar.

  • Booking Process: Homestays are booked directly through verified community tourism networks or trusted aggregators specializing in offbeat Sikkim circuits with a 30% advance deposit. All chosen properties feature verified host families and secure environments tailored for women travelers.

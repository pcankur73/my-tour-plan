⛰️ Silk Route Expedition (Sikkim & West Bengal)

6 Days Offbeat Scenic Circuit Tour Plan

📅 Dates: 19/10/2026 – 24/10/2026 👥 Group Size: 6 Persons 💰 Budget: ₹7,000 / Head

Day 1: Arrival at NJP to Icche Gaon

NJP / Siliguri ➔ Kalimpong ➔ Icche Gaon (88 km / 4 hrs)

Pickup from NJP station in the morning. Drive towards Icche Gaon, a serene eco-village perched amidst pine forests. Check into your homestay, refresh, and enjoy a gentle nature walk.

📍 Highlights
  • Silent Valley sunset views
  • Panoramic view of Kanchenjunga
  • Pine forest village walk
🍴 Meals & Lodging Lunch, Evening Tea & Snacks, Dinner. Overnight stay in Icche Gaon Homestay.

Day 2: Icche Gaon to Sillery Gaon & Reshikhola / Aritar

Icche Gaon ➔ Sillery Gaon ➔ Pedong ➔ Reshikhola / Aritar (45 km / 3 hrs)

Witness Kanchenjunga at sunrise. Drive to Sillery Gaon and trek to Ramitey Dara to view the 14 turns of the Teesta River. Visit Damsang Fort ruins before heading down to Reshikhola or uphill to Aritar.

📍 Highlights
  • Ramitey Dara Viewpoint
  • Historical Damsang Fort ruins
  • Lampokhari Lake (Aritar) & Reshi River
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast at Icche Gaon; Lunch, Snacks, & Dinner at Reshikhola/Aritar Homestay.

Day 3: Reshikhola / Aritar to Padamchen via Rongli

Aritar / Reshikhola ➔ Rongli ➔ Kuekhola Waterfalls ➔ Padamchen (40 km / 2.5 hrs)

Halt at Rongli Market to obtain inner-line Silk Route permits. Ascend through dense Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary forests toward Padamchen, stopping at Kuekhola Waterfalls along the way.

📍 Highlights
  • Rongli Permit Office & Market
  • Kuekhola Waterfalls
  • Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary dense forests
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Padamchen Homestay.

Day 4: Padamchen to Zuluk & The Silk Route High Pass

Padamchen ➔ Zuluk ➔ Thambi ➔ Lungthung ➔ Nathang ➔ Kupup (60 km excursion)

Early morning drive winding up the famous 32-curve Zig-Zag Road to Thambi View Point. Proceed through Lungthung, Tukla Valley, Old Baba Mandir, and reach Elephant Lake (Kupup Lake).

📍 Highlights
  • Thambi View Point (11,600 ft) & Zig-Zag Road
  • Lungthung & Tukla Valley War Memorial
  • Old Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir
  • Elephant/Kupup Lake & World's Highest Golf Course
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Zuluk Homestay.

Day 5: Zuluk to Snow Point / High Altitude Pass (Zero Point Excursion)

Zuluk ➔ Nathang Valley ➔ Eagle's Nest / Snow Point ➔ Zuluk / Padamchen

Drive to the highest snow-accumulation points along the Silk Route circuit (Nathang Valley & Eagle's Nest Bunker viewpoints). Spend the morning in the high-altitude terrain before returning for a relaxed evening.

📍 Highlights
  • Nathang Valley High Altitude Pass
  • Eagle's Nest Bunker viewpoint
  • Snow fields & high-altitude bogs
🍴 Meals & Lodging Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, & Dinner at Zuluk/Padamchen Homestay.

Day 6: Padamchen / Zuluk to NJP Station

Padamchen ➔ Rongli ➔ Rangpo / Sevoke ➔ NJP (100 km / 4.5 hrs)

After breakfast, check out from the homestay. Drive downhill through Rangpo and the Teesta Valley back to NJP/Siliguri for your return train journey.

📍 Sights En Route Teesta River Valley, Rangpo Checkpost, Coronation Bridge.
🍴 Meals Breakfast at Homestay; Lunch on the way near Rangpo / Teesta Bazar.

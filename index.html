Trip Snapshot

The lowest-cost sensible structure is to use the daily 1A Pune–Visakhapatnam and Visakhapatnam–Pune services, stay near the railway station, use a compact AC car only where it saves multiple transfers, use a local Araku sightseeing cab for the hill circuit, and return to Vizag before the overnight train.

Why 18520 is used instead of the faster Konark

  • Konark Express 11019 is faster at about 21h 40m but does not currently list 1A on the Pune–Vizag route.
  • 18520 is daily and explicitly offers 1A, matching the requested class.
  • 18520 has no pantry; therefore the four-times-daily food budget below deliberately includes train meals.

Booking Sequence — Do This in Order

  1. Book 1A trains first: 18520 on 17 Oct and 18519 on 22 Oct. Keep the final fare screenshot/PNR.
  2. Lock Araku accommodation: ask Krishna Tara for one room for 3 adults for 19–21 Oct and get the total after tax in writing.
  3. Lock Vizag accommodation: request the lowest available AC/basic rooms for 18–19 Oct and 21–22 Oct; verify whether one room can legally house all three adults.
  4. Book the 19 Oct car: ask for an AC Ertiga or similar, Vizag pickup → Borra Caves → Araku, with driver allowance, tolls, parking, extra-hour rate and luggage capacity written down.
  5. Book the Araku local cab: use a flat 2-day circuit if you want the complete valley, or ₹4,000 Side A + ₹3,000 Side B if booked separately.
  6. Recheck the AC bus: 7–10 days before 21 Oct. If a direct AC service is not genuinely available, use the published APSRTC bus or ask the Araku cab operator for a station/bus-stand transfer.
  7. Keep ₹3,000 contingency: October is a peak period and hill-weather delays can alter the road schedule.
Best overall low-cost configuration: 1A train both ways + Hotel Lakshmi Grand/Akshaya in Vizag + Krishna Tara in Araku + AC Ertiga for Borra/Araku + Araku local flat-rate cab + public bus only for the Araku→Vizag transfer if the live service is suitable.

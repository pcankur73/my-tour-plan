What to book, in order

  1. Rail first: reserve 3 × 2A seats Kolkata → Visakhapatnam for 22 Oct and 3 × 2A seats Visakhapatnam → Kolkata for the night of 25 Oct.
  2. Araku hotel next: call Ushodaya and Krishna Tara and ask specifically for a 3-adult room for 24 Oct, including all taxes.
  3. Visakhapatnam hotel: ask Shree Lakshmi for a triple/family room for 23 Oct and confirm early check-in after the train arrival.
  4. Car: call at least three local operators and ask for a written all-inclusive quote for 24–25 Oct: Araku local sightseeing + Borra Caves + drop to Vizag on 25 Oct.
  5. Bus backup: do not depend on luxury AC. Keep APSRTC Ultra Deluxe / Express as the fallback. Official APSRTC information confirms frequent Visakhapatnam–Araku services, while current online listings show the lower-cost route fares. citeturn0search0turn1search9
  6. Meals: budget ₹250/person/day and carry dry food for the trains.
